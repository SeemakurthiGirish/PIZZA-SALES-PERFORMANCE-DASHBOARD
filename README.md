# 🍕 Pizza Sales Performance Dashboard 📊

An interactive Pizza Sales Performance Dashboard built in Power BI, designed to analyze sales performance, revenue trends, customer ordering patterns, and pizza-level performance. The dashboard provides business insights into revenue, orders, pizza quantity sold, product categories, sizes, and sales trends over time.

---

## 🔍 Overview

This dashboard analyzes **21,334 orders** and **49,559 pizzas sold**, generating approximately **$817.62K in total revenue**.

The dashboard enables businesses to monitor overall sales performance, identify top and underperforming pizza products, understand customer ordering behavior, and analyze revenue trends across months, days, pizza categories, and sizes.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- **Monthly Revenue Trend** – Tracks revenue performance from January to December
- **Sales Distribution by Category** – Compares Classic, Supreme, Chicken, and Veggie pizza categories
- **Sales Distribution by Pizza Size** – Analyzes sales contribution across Large, Medium, Small, X-Large, and XX-Large sizes
- **Orders by Day of Week** – Identifies the days with the highest and lowest order volumes
- **Pizza Performance Analysis** – Highlights the best and worst-performing pizzas based on revenue, orders, and quantity sold
- **Pizza Sales by Size & Category** – Compares pizza quantities across different sizes and categories
- **Revenue by Pizza Name** – Uses a treemap to visualize revenue contribution by individual pizza
- **Interactive Filters** – Allows users to filter the dashboard by month, pizza category, and pizza size

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- The Thai Chicken Pizza generated the highest revenue among all pizza products.
- The Classic Deluxe Pizza recorded the highest number of orders and the highest quantity sold.
- The Brie Carre Pizza was the lowest-performing pizza based on revenue, orders, and quantity sold.
- July recorded the highest monthly revenue at approximately $72.6K, while September recorded the lowest at approximately $64.2K.
- Classic pizzas contributed the largest share of category sales at 26.91%, followed by Supreme (25.46%), Chicken (23.95%), and Veggie (23.68%).
- Large pizzas dominated sales with approximately 45.89% of total pizza sales, followed by Medium (30.49%) and Small (21.77%).
- Thursday recorded the highest number of orders at approximately 3,535, while Saturday had the lowest with approximately 2,624 orders.
- The business generated $817.62K revenue from more than 21K orders, with customers purchasing an average of 2.32 pizzas per order.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open "Pizza_Sales_Performance_Dashboard.pbix" using Power BI Desktop.
3. Load the required dataset.
4. Use the interactive filters on the left side of the dashboard to analyze:
   - Monthly performance
   - Pizza category
   - Pizza size
5. Explore the visuals to identify sales trends, top-performing products, and areas for improvement.
